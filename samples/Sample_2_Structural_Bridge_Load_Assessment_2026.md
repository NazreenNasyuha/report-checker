Doc Ref: CIV-2026-NRV2  |  Prepared by: Bridge Structural Group  |  Classification: Engineering Audit 

# **1. Dead & Live Load Superposition Summary** 

This structural assessment evaluates Span B of the North River Viaduct according to highway bridge design code [1] and seismic retrofit guidelines [2]. Ultrasonic weld measurment confirmed that primary box-girder flanges meet the required structural tolerence. 

Unfactored vertical reaction force at Pier P3 was computed by summing dead, wearing surface, and live traffic loads: 1850.0 + 420.0 + 680.0 = 3010.0 kN at mid-span bearing pads. 

Peak wind gust velocity across the deck reached 28.5 mps during storm monitoring, while backup de-icing heaters consumed 18.4 MW/h over the winter test period. Geotechnical borehole logs are included in Appendix A, and fatigue crack propagation curves are detailed in Appendix D alongside finite-element verification in citation [5].
